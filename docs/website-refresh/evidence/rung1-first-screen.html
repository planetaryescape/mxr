A local mail runtime for you and your agents.

Put your whole mail history to work.

A Gmail connector gives your agent access to Gmail. mxr syncs connected accounts into a local mailbox with common mail operations you, your scripts and your agents can use together. Search synced history, build workflows, and review prepared work in mxr.
