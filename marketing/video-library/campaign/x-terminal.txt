mxr started because I wanted to write email in Vim.

It has a Vim-friendly TUI, a pipeable CLI and fast local search. The daemon keeps syncing when I close the interface.

Try it: https://mxr.sh/
If you like it, star it or contribute: https://github.com/planetaryescape/mxr
