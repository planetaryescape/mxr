The ten-item plan in `docs/blueprint/21-web-experience.md` has shipped
(v0.6.43 to v0.6.46), apart from what only a person can do. What remains:

## Needs code

- **B3:** conversation unstar can leave the star set, and `u` can't undo a
  star (`docs/issues/web-star-undo-gaps.md`). Fix in progress on
  `fix/unstar`.

## Needs BK

- **E2, the dogfooding log:** five working days of real use, logged in
  `docs/dogfooding-log.md`. Until it exists, every section-A criterion is
  capped at 2.
- **C3, the copy review:** read the app's copy across screens (backlog,
  empty and error states) and note anything that nags or scolds. The log has
  a checklist.
- **A11, gist accuracy:** check whether the gists and asks on your desk
  match the emails. Coverage is measured; correctness isn't. The log has a
  checklist.
- **B4, B9:** sound on real speakers, and swipes on a real phone.

After the log exists, an independent grader re-scores section A for 3.
