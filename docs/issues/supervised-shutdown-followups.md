# Supervision must bound shutdown outside the daemon

S08a is released in v0.6.64 from `6ed0554c24df5bfbb99aaaebec5fdfab38254f50`; the [installed proof](../build-log/daemon-portability/evidence/s08a-installed-v0.6.64.json) records successful installer and Homebrew stop/restart journeys. The original audit used `339ef847e61a7645716b595fc2ae18a8df927793`, based on `3d17b1600a9db36b9f062bc4e2ca6997d8a9c562`. Final S08a candidate `62421fe6d87692e2d2c780713b3bb49c98eb7d09` supersedes the handshake finding below. Remaining observations are source checks unless runtime evidence is named.

At the original candidate, `AppState::shutdown_runtime_tasks` awaited the search and semantic shutdown handshakes before creating the runtime-task deadline (`crates/daemon/src/state.rs:1578`). The search handshake awaited command admission and the response without its own timeout (`crates/search/src/service.rs:279`). The strict S08a smoke later exposed missing relationship/contacts shutdown requests. Candidate `62421fe6` includes those requests, bounds all four service handshakes with one deadline, and cancels unfinished registered async tasks. It does not make already-running blocking work cancellable.

At candidate `62421fe6d87692e2d2c780713b3bb49c98eb7d09`, the stop/restart acceptance check permits cancellation of unfinished relationship analytics during an active sync and requires a clean idle drain after restart. That proves acknowledged mail survives; it does not prove the cancelled analytics queue is replayed. S08c must exercise analytics recovery or document the explicit rebuild needed before promising uninterrupted classification across supervised restarts. The queue is process-local in `crates/relationship/src/service.rs:52-55`; do not infer replay from the persisted message count.

The [recovery-path audit](../build-log/daemon-portability/evidence/s08c-analytics-recovery-audit.md) at main `2c55a735` distinguishes direction/contact-table repair from relationship style, summary and commitments. `mxr doctor --rebuild-analytics` does not rebuild relationship profiles. `mxr profile <email> --rebuild` requests one contact's rebuild; summary and commitment failures are logged separately, so a successful command alone does not prove all derived fields were repaired. Changed-message sync, provider deletion and initial AI draft generation can also trigger contact work. None of the inspected startup paths reconstructs the lost queue. S08c needs a no-new-mail restart case and separate assertions for mail, search and relationship data.

S08c must document and exercise a supervisor stop deadline followed by forced termination of its owned process. Test the stalled-worker case on Linux and verify restart/recovery through the released operation contracts. Do not describe repeated SIGINT/SIGTERM as escalation: once shutdown begins, the accept loop no longer consumes them.

The shared `DaemonGuard` sends SIGTERM and waits for socket removal without checking child exit or escalating (`crates/test-support/src/daemon.rs:47`). Its reusable cleanup should eventually own a child handle, wait/reap it, and escalate only for that owned child. Do not add process-wide killing. The new S08a fixture can fix its own reaped-PID bookkeeping without widening this stage into every test harness.

Ordinary CLI autostart during a supervised daemon's drain can also start a background daemon outside the supervisor after broken-daemon recovery (`crates/daemon/src/server.rs:816`, `1499`, `1543`). That behavior predates S08a. The headless recipe needs a deliberate supervised-client policy and a test, rather than claiming signal support alone completes supervised deployment.
