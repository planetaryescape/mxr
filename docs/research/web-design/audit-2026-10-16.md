# mxr web: visual design audit (2026-10-16)

The app was captured on 2026-10-16 from the Vite dev server at `http://127.0.0.1:5198`, against an isolated daemon with fake mail (daemon 0.6.105, protocol 18, per `41-diagnostics-dark-1440.png`). The server ran from a worktree on branch `fix/assistant-outstanding` at `eb873e036`, with a clean tree. `git diff --stat eb873e036 origin/main -- apps/web/src` is empty (`origin/main` = `a669d2f3a`), so the web code here matches main. All code paths below are relative to `apps/web/src/` at that SHA.

The 79 screenshots were taken locally and are not committed (they are fake mail, but large); re-run the audit with agent-browser against `mxr demo` to reproduce them. Names follow `NN-screen-theme-width.png`. "Dark" means the System theme on a dark OS, which resolves to Midnight. I looked at every file listed here before writing about it.

## Per screen

### Now (front page)

Screenshots: `01-now-dark-1440.png`, `20-sidebar-collapsed-dark-1440.png` (two-column at a wider main), `50-now-light-1440.png`, `58-now-paper-1440.png`, `59-now-eclipse-1440.png`, `60-now-dark-390.png`.

- **Where the eye lands:** the sentence "Friday night. 12 people waiting on you, 3 things to act on." It's the largest regular-weight text and it's useful. Next is the selected Quinn Dev row, the only filled surface with the cyan left rule.
- **Hierarchy:** primary is the summary sentence plus the PEOPLE rows. Secondary is DUE SOON and UPDATES. The noise is the "Since 01:02: 0 arrived" disclosure (it reports zero, styled as a bare native `▶` summary), "and 9 more in Messages" floating top-right of the section, and "Not now: Reading 1 this week".
- **Busy or equal weight:** each PEOPLE row stacks three lines of underlined links: "Also in To do: …", then "New sender. Keep in Messages? Messages Updates Reading Block". That gives five underlined links per row, all the same weight, competing with the row's own name and subject. The underline treatment makes the page read like a Wikipedia article, not an inbox. DUE SOON rows switch to a *bordered chip* for the same "Also in …" idea. PEOPLE uses an underlined link, DUE SOON uses a boxed chip, so one concept has two styles on one screen.
- **Title:** "Now" is small (about 17-18px) and sits on one baseline with its grey tagline, so the page title is barely bigger than body text. The summary sentence under it is visually louder than the title.
- **Section headings** (PEOPLE, DUE SOON, UPDATES) are letter-spaced mono caps at 11px, with no rule and little space above. They separate content weakly. The rest of the app uses them inconsistently (see cross-cutting).
- **Actions:** reply and check icons appear only on the selected row, as hover icons. Unselected rows show a date instead ("Wed"). The first row has no date at all. Updates offers an outlined "Open g u" button next to a borderless "Let go of this digest A". Primary and secondary buttons look unrelated.
- **Why it's here:** shown as prose ("New sender. Keep in Messages?") and as "Also in X" cross-links. There's no consistent "because …" line, unlike Reading and Archive, which use "Here because: …".
- **Missing:** a visual summary of the day (counts per mode as glanceable tiles or a strip), a clear "you're done" state, and any typographic scale step between title, summary and rows.
- **Phone (`60`):** the "Also in To do: … act by Fr" line is clipped mid-word with no ellipsis, as is "act" on the next row. The kbd hint for Updates is hidden here, but kbd chips appear elsewhere on phone.

### Messages (people list and person page)

Screenshots: `02-messages-dark-1440.png`, `03-messages-person-dark-1440.png`, `03b-messages-person-multitopic-dark-1440.png`, `51-messages-person-light-1440.png`, `61-messages-dark-390.png`, `62-messages-person-dark-390.png`, `21b-assistant-messages-answer-dark-1440.png`.

- **Where the eye lands:** the cyan unread dots running down the right edge of the list, then the bold names. In the person pane, it's the message bubbles.
- **Hierarchy:** the list rows are the primary content. The noise is the "New sender. Keep in Messages? Messages Updates Reading Block" block, which appears on almost every row (Pat, Diana/Eve, Kim/Liam, Grace…) and doubles row height. Grace Admin's row has six lines: quote, three "Also in …" lines, and the screener prompt.
- **Equal weight:** snippet, "Also in" lines and the screener prompt all use the same 13px fg-2. Nothing tells you which line is the message and which is metadata.
- **Empty detail state (`02`):** a lone `›` chevron icon with "Choose a conversation". The icon reads as a broken arrow rather than an illustration.
- **Person page:** this is the most considered screen in the app. It has a name header, a relationship sentence, a topics list with turn state, chat-style bubbles, and a docked composer bar with clear actions (Reply all, Got it ., Done here e, To do t). But:
  - Copy reads oddly: "Pat has written once since 2026."
  - "new" sits alone at top-right with no styling. It isn't a badge, a tag or a date.
  - The teaching hint (`03b`) is a saturated teal callout box. It's the loudest element on the page and covers the gap between topics and the conversation.
  - Bubbles are surface-filled rectangles with no sender alignment. Pat's message and RustConf's message look the same, so "chat" is half-implied.
- **Phone (`61`, `62`):** works structurally, with a back arrow and docked composer. The kbd chip `r` shows on a touch device, and the screener prompt wraps across two lines on every row.

## Cross-cutting findings

### The visual language as it actually exists

Source: `styles/tokens.css` (510 lines), `styles/base.css`, `styles/app.css` (695 lines), `styles/shadcn.css`. There's no Tailwind config file; Tailwind v4 is configured in CSS through `@theme`.

**Type.**
- **Family:** one family, Recursive Variable (`--font-sans`, `--font-heading` and `--font-mono` all point at it). Mono is the `MONO` axis via `--rec-ui: "MONO" 0` and `--rec-mono: "MONO" 1`.
- **Axes:** the loaded file is `@fontsource-variable/recursive/mono.css`, with only the `wght` and `MONO` axes, so the `CASL` and `slnt` voices that root `DESIGN.md` describes for the marketing site aren't available in the app.
- **Scale** (`tokens.css` L21-30): 2xs 11px, xs 12px, sm 13px (body), base 14px, md 15px, lg 17px, xl 20px, 2xl 24px, 3xl 32px, 4xl 48px.
- **Actual use** (`rg` count over non-test `.tsx`): xs 329, 2xs 302, sm 248, md 28, base 19, lg 9, xl 5, 2xl 4, 3xl 1, plus one arbitrary `text-[28px]`. Almost the whole product lives at 11-13px, and only about 20 places use anything above 15px. That's why every screen feels the same weight: there's no display tier in use.
- **Eyebrows:** 47 `uppercase` uses, with four tracking values (`tracking-[0.12em]` ×18, `tracking-wide` ×16, `tracking-wider` ×10, `tracking-widest` ×2, `tracking-[0.08em]` ×2). Section labels are 11px mono caps in modes ("PEOPLE", "YOUR TURN"), 11px sans caps in the palette ("MAIL", "COMPOSE"), and mono caps with a rule in Settings and Analytics.

**Colour** (Midnight, `tokens.css` L166-236).
- **Surfaces:** sidebar `#080f17`, background `#0d161f`, surface and card `#17202a`, elevated and popover `#1f2a35`, muted `#1c2632`, subtle `#232f3c`.
- **Text tiers:** foreground `#e2e9f0`, fg-2 `#bbc5d1`, muted-foreground `#99a6b5`, subtle-foreground `#8795a5`, faint `#657383`. That's five text tiers, too many to tell apart at 12-13px, and in the screenshots fg-2 and muted are hard to distinguish.
- **Borders:** `#2b3742`, strong `#424f5c`, input `#687887`.
- **Accent:** primary and ring `#5cd5fc` / `#54ddff` (cyan), primary-muted `#01394a`.
- **Status:** destructive `#fd8a8c`, success `#68d7a1`, warning, vip and star `#f8ca65`, pending `#baaaf8`.
- **Charts:** six colours.
- **Other themes:** Light (pale blue paper, cyan `#006f93`), Eclipse (OKLCH violet-black, magenta `oklch(80% .13 330)`), Paper (warm off-white, rust `hsl(14 75% 38%)`).
- **Contrast:** an increased-contrast override per theme.
- **Issue:** in Paper, the rust primary is also the unread and badge colour, so sidebar counts read as error red (`58`).

**Radii:** sm 3, md 5, lg 7, xl 10, 2xl 14 (L34-38). In use: `rounded-md` ×134, `rounded` ×59, `rounded-full` ×56, `rounded-sm` ×25, `rounded-lg` ×19, `rounded-xl` ×8. In practice that's square-ish controls plus fully round pills (Archive filters, avatars, chips), with no rule for when to use which.

**Spacing:** the Tailwind 4px base (`--spacing: 0.25rem`) only. There's no named rhythm. Row heights are tokens (compact 34, regular 56, comfortable 72). Shell sizes are sidebar 248/56, top bar 48, status bar 28, right rail 380, list 420. Frames are list 72rem, reader 61.25rem, wide 112rem. The reading measure is 62ch.

**Shadows:** **no shadow tokens**. Ad hoc use: `shadow-sm` ×10, `shadow-lg` ×7, `shadow-2xl` ×6, `shadow-xl` ×3, `shadow-md` ×3, plus raw `box-shadow: -16px 0 40px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / .45)` for drawers (`app.css` L184, L260). Elevation is mostly carried by surface colour, which is fine on dark but flat on Light (`50`).

**Motion** (L61-76): fast 120ms, base 180ms, slow 280ms, scene 1800ms ("low tide"), pulse 1500ms. Easing is out `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`, in-out, and drawer. The policy is good and written down: no animation on repeated keyboard actions, and reduced motion keeps fades. Motion was barely visible during the audit.

**Design doc:** root `DESIGN.md` (254 lines) is "derived from `site/src/styles/custom.css`", which is the marketing site. It specifies display weights of 800, folios (`§ 01`), section rules and the `CASL` axis. **None of that has reached the app.** `tokens.css` L8 says the theme "follows DESIGN.md", but only the palette and font made it across. The app has no design document of its own.

### Shared components and where screens diverge

- **Three page frames:**
  - `components/ModeFrame.tsx` (`ModeFrame`, `ModeHeader`, `PageTitle`, `ModeColumns`) is used by now, messages, todo, updates, reading, archive, mailbox, thread and modes. Its header puts the title and tagline on one line.
  - `components/Page.tsx` (`Page`, `PageSection`, `PageTabs`) is used by analytics, diagnostics, settings, rules, drafts, accounts, screener, jobs, invites, deliveries and activity. Its header stacks the title over the tagline and adds tabs and a top-right action.
  - Bespoke: snoozed, owed, reply-queue and mailbox lists use a title-only bar. Focus has its own header ("FOCUS & REPLY" eyebrow and "Leave esc"). Search has its own big-input header.
  - Result: header heights of about 92px, 103px, 125px and 147px depending on the screen (compare `17`, `08`, `31`, `15`).
- **Empty states:** `PageEmpty` (27 uses, `components/PageParts.tsx` L64) is top-aligned with an icon, title and body. Snoozed uses a local `Centered` (`features/snoozed/SnoozedRoute.tsx` L79), as do owed and reply-queue, and it's vertically centred. Mailbox has `EmptyLens` (`features/mailbox/MailView.tsx` L150), also centred. Focus has `FocusFinish` (large left-aligned heading). Now has `NowEmpty`. The assistant has its own `EmptyState`. That's at least five empty-state designs.
- **Errors:** `PageError` (`PageParts.tsx` L36), `RequestError`, `NotFoundPage`, `ErrorBoundary` with "Try again", and local "Try again" buttons in Messages, Updates, Arrivals and Reading routes. The offline banner and recovery card stack.
- **Keys:** `KeyChip` (93 uses), `KeyLine` (5), raw `<kbd>` (11), plus plain-text key hints in the search footer. Key hints appear in four places: the status-bar footer, an in-page legend under lists (To do, Reading, Archive, Waiting on), row hover (Updates), and inside buttons ("Open email ↵", "Unsubscribe D").
- **Buttons:** 287 `<Button>` and 139 raw `<button>`. Variants in use are ghost 105, outline 102, destructive 20, secondary 7, warning 5, success 1. "Default" (filled primary) is implicit and rare, so pages have no consistent primary action. Compose's Send is filled. "Open email" is outlined on To do, a ghost box in Updates, and a text link ("The email o") on Archive.
- **Rows:** about six row designs:
  - Inbox wide row (`09`) and inbox narrow three-line row (`10`)
  - Messages person row (`02`)
  - To do task row with checkbox (`04`)
  - Updates two-column row (`05b`)
  - Reading card (`06`)
  - Archive ledger row (`08`)
  - Screener row with button group (`30`)
  - `RuledRow` for tool pages (`31`, `36`)
- **"Also in" and "Why it's here":** "Also in X: …" is an underlined link (Now PEOPLE, Messages, thread), a bordered chip (Now DUE SOON) or plain fg-2 text (Messages list). "Here because: …" is used in Reading, Updates and Archive, but not in Now, Messages, To do or Inbox, where the reason is a question ("New sender. Keep in Messages?") or absent.
- **Selection:** a subtle fill with a 2px cyan left rule (Now, To do, Inbox, Reading card, Updates), a saturated teal fill (Screener, Settings nav, Subscriptions segment), or a filled pill (Archive "All", Analytics "Sender").
- **Segmented controls:** a square toggle group with a filled cyan selected segment (Subscriptions, Analytics, compose Rich/Markdown), a square group with a subtle selected segment (thread Formatted/Reader/Plain, Search Exact/Hybrid/Meaning), round outlined pills (Archive), and underline tabs (Screener, Deliveries, Diagnostics, Analytics).

### Top 15 design problems, ranked by how much they hurt the whole product

1. **No typographic hierarchy.** About 880 of about 945 sized text uses are 11-13px (counts above). Page titles are about 17-20px, inline with taglines. Every screen reads as one grey-blue block, and it's the main reason the product feels "built, not designed" (`01`, `04`, `05`, `09`).
2. **The product contradicts itself on who is waiting.** Now says "12 people waiting on you", the thread says "you owe a reply since Wed", but Focus says "Nobody is waiting on a reply from you" and Owed says "You don't owe anyone a reply". Waiting on says "Nobody is waiting on you". Screener says 28 while its badge says 17 (`01`, `10`, `16`, `33`, `43`, `30`). Nothing in the UI explains the different definitions, so trust breaks.
3. **Three page frames and four header heights.** ModeFrame, Page and bespoke headers place the title, tagline and actions differently. Moving between modes and tools feels like moving between apps (`08` vs `31` vs `17` vs `15`).
4. **Inline link soup in the core lists.** "Also in …" and "New sender. Keep in Messages? Messages Updates Reading Block" appear as underlined text on most Now and Messages rows, often doubling row height. They're the loudest repeated element in the two most-used screens (`01`, `02`).
5. **Phone navigation is incomplete and wrong.** Updates and Archive have no tab and light up "Find". Settings, Screener, Snoozed, Drafts and Labels aren't reachable. The Screener layout breaks, and kbd chips show on touch (`64`, `67`, `70`, `72`).
6. **Keyboard hints are everywhere, in four formats.** The footer bar, in-page legends, row-hover chips and chips inside buttons appear together on one screen (Reading `06`/`07`, Archive `08`). The hints compete with content instead of sitting in one predictable place.
7. **No consistent primary action.** Filled cyan appears on Send, "+ New draft", "+ New rule", "+ Add account", "Try again" and "Go to Now". Everything else is outline or ghost. Mode screens have no visually primary action at all (`04`, `05`, `06`).
8. **Five-plus empty-state designs** (top-aligned `PageEmpty`, centred `Centered` and `EmptyLens`, the large `FocusFinish`, the chevron "Choose a conversation", the double empty in Waiting on) (`17`, `32`, `16`, `02`, `43`).
9. **Error states are developer-facing and offer the wrong fix.** "Try again" for an invalid id, raw `invalid thread id: …`, stacked amber and grey banners with a misleading "protocol upgrade" message, a blank content pane when offline, and a skeleton that never resolves (`27`, `28`, `29a`, `29e`).
10. **The sidebar is a 30-item flat list with a junk-drawer MORE group.** Look-alike icons when collapsed, and items that appear or disappear with connection state (`19`, `20`, `29e`).
11. **Selection and segmented-control styles vary.** Saturated teal (Screener, Settings) vs subtle fill and rule (everywhere else); round pills vs square toggles vs underline tabs for the same job (`30`, `15`, `08`, `13c`).
12. **"Why it's here" is inconsistent.** It's a clear "Here because:" line in Reading, Updates and Archive, but a question, a cross-link or nothing in Now, Messages, To do and Inbox, which are the screens where it matters most.
13. **Teaching hints are louder than content.** Saturated teal popovers stay pinned after you move on (`03b`, `05b`, `05c`). The key help shows the same description twice and includes TUI-parity notes (`14a`).
14. **Elevation and shape are undefined.** There are no shadow tokens, radius use is ad hoc (md, base, full and lg mixed), and only Reading uses a card. The floating composer has no scrim and sits on live list rows (`11`, `06`, `50`).
15. **Copy and units are inconsistent.** "49 messages" vs "2 of 35", "1 result" vs facets totalling 2, "since 2026", "Reply later" vs "Reply queue", and breadcrumbs "LLM" and "Keybindings" vs nav labels "Language model" and "Keyboard" (`09`, `10`, `13c`, `03`, `15b`, `15c`).

### What a design team would add

- A type scale that's actually used: a display tier for page titles, a distinct section heading style, and one eyebrow style with one tracking value.
- One page-header component with fixed slots for title, one-line purpose, a primary action, a secondary overflow and optional tabs.
- One row anatomy with slots for leading (avatar, checkbox), primary line, secondary line, a reason line and trailing (meta, actions), with variants per mode.
- One "reason" component used everywhere, and cross-mode presence shown as small mode glyphs or chips instead of underlined sentences.
- A key-hint policy: one place per screen, hidden on touch.
- Empty, loading and error patterns, each with one layout and a matching skeleton per frame.
- Elevation tokens (raised, overlay, drawer) and a radius rule (controls vs chips vs avatars).
- A navigation IA that groups MORE into Mail folders, Views and Tools, gives the phone a "More" sheet, and makes active-tab logic cover every mode.
- An app-specific `DESIGN.md`, because the root one documents the marketing site.
