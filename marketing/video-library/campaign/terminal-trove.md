Name: mxr

URL: https://mxr.sh/

Source: https://github.com/planetaryescape/mxr

Description:
A local-first email client with a Vim-friendly TUI and a pipeable CLI. It syncs Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and IMAP accounts, stores mail in SQLite and searches it with Tantivy. A long-running daemon keeps sync going when the TUI closes.

Language: Rust

Platforms: macOS, Linux

License: MIT OR Apache-2.0
